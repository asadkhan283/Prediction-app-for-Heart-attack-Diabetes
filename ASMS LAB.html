Diabetes interpretation

  • Fasting glucose and HbA1c are more clinically meaningful than the original skin-thickness/insulin-only approach.
  • BMI, waist circumference, family history, blood pressure, triglycerides and HDL provide additional metabolic context.
  • The score shown by this prototype is not a clinically validated probability and must not be advertised as “X% accurate.”
Diagnostic distinction: A risk/screening score is not the same as a diabetes diagnosis. Diagnostic interpretation requires appropriate laboratory testing and clinical context.

How to obtain a real accuracy value: train/lock the model on a development dataset, then evaluate an untouched test cohort. Report AUROC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, Brier score, calibration slope/intercept and a calibration plot. Accuracy alone is insufficient for a medical prediction model.

Technical Documentation

Current prototype architecture.

  • Frontend: standalone HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
  • Cardiovascular: 2013 ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equation reference implementation for 10-year hard ASCVD.
  • Diabetes: expanded transparent screening index; not a trained/validated clinical model.
  • Data: no server/database is required for this local prototype.
  • Safety: input range checks, prior-ASCVD warning, acute-symptom warning and international-use caveats.

Safety & Clinical Limitations

Read before interpreting any output.

Clinical limitation: This HTML prototype is not an approved medical device and has not been prospectively validated for diagnosis, treatment, triage or emergency decision-making.

Acute symptoms: severe/persistent chest pressure or pain, shortness of breath, fainting, sudden weakness, or other concerning symptoms require urgent medical assessment rather than reliance on a browser prediction.

International use: published U.S.-derived cardiovascular equations can behave differently in other populations. Country-specific validation/recalibration is required before clinical deployment in Pakistan or elsewhere.
